Behind the radar

Find the signal before you create.

NicheRadar turns public YouTube Shorts metadata into a focused picture of what is gaining traction, which creators are outperforming their size, and whether recent results suggest an opportunity worth researching.

The project

YouTube Shorts research without the guesswork.

NicheRadar combines focused searches, real performance statistics and transparent analytical signals into one ranked report.

01

Shape the search

A niche is expanded into focused search angles that the user can review, remove or replace before analysis.

02

Compare real performance

Public views, upload age and channel size are converted into comparable signals such as views per day and subscriber multiplier.

03

Spot unusual traction

Breakout and exceptional-performance highlights reveal videos performing unusually well for their channels.

What it provides

A focused niche snapshot

  • Up to 50 high-view candidates, displayed by views per day
  • Breakout and exceptional-performance highlights
  • Niche virality and confidence scores
  • Channel, thumbnail and performance metadata

Built with

An end-to-end product stack

Python FastAPI SQLAlchemy SQLite Groq YouTube Data API HTML CSS JavaScript Podman

Scope

Metadata intelligence, not a guarantee of success.

NicheRadar analyses public metadata rather than video content. Its scores are research signals designed to support creator decisions, not predictions of future performance. NicheRadar is not affiliated with YouTube.
